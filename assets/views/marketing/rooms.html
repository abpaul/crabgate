The fee on every ticket adds up fast

Take a room with 150 seats and a night a week, 40 weeks a year. That's 6,000 tickets.

At Ticket Tailor's published pay-as-you-go rate, that's about £4,300 a year. At Eventbrite UK's published rate on a £15 ticket, it's about £9,800.

Either that comes out of your takings, or your regulars pay it at checkout, every night, on top of the ticket. Over five years, that's between £21,500 and £49,000 leaving the same room.

And the fee isn't buying you an audience. You built the list. You fill the room.

Questions from room owners

Do I have to move every event at once?
No. Put your next night on CrabGate and leave the rest where they are. Move the others when you're ready.
What does it cost besides {{ price }}?
Only your card processor's fee. Today that's Stripe, and we add nothing to it.
Can my shop be on my own web address?
Not yet. Your shop lives at yourname.crabgate.com, and you link to it from your own site. Your own domain is on our list. If it matters to you, tell us.
Can I sell numbered seats?
Yes. Enter a section, a row, and the seat labels, and buyers pick their seats from the list. A map of the room is on our list.
What if it isn't for me?
The setup call is free, and you pay only when your event goes on sale. If that first event doesn't go well, email Paul within 30 days of paying and he'll refund the {{ price }}.
What if my payment to CrabGate fails?
Your shop keeps working for a 14-day grace period, so you have time to sort it out. If payment is not restored, your subscription lapses and ticket sales stop.
