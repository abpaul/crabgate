Eventbrite in the US

Published US rates list a service fee of 3.7% plus $1.79 per paid ticket and payment processing of 2.9% of the order total, including service fees and tax. Free tickets have no Eventbrite ticketing fee.

Questions before you switch

Will CrabGate find buyers for me?
CrabGate is a hosted ticket shop, not an event discovery marketplace. You bring the audience. If Eventbrite discovery brings you buyers, include that channel in your decision.
Can I use my own domain, logo, and colors?
Not yet. Your hosted shop uses a CrabGate address. If branding is part of your decision, tell us what you need.
What should I check for recurring workshops?
Check dates, capacity, orders, and buyer instructions for each workshop. Read CrabGate for workshops and ask about the workflow your next date needs before you plan a series.
Does no CrabGate ticket fee mean no payment fees?
No. Stripe processing still applies. The {{ price }} yearly subscription is paid by the organizer.
