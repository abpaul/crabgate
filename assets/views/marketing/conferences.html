Questions from conference organisers

What if CrabGate goes away?
If we stop offering CrabGate, we'll give you at least 90 days' notice. You can export your data while live hosted data is available. Export it before we delete your live hosted data, 90 days after your subscription lapses.
What does it cost besides {{ price }}?
Only your card processor's fee. Today that's Stripe, and we add nothing to it.
Can one person buy tickets for a team?
Yes. A buyer can put several tickets in one order and change the attendee names on the order page until the conference starts.
Do we have to move our old data?
No. Start with your next conference. Last year's orders can stay where they are.
What if it isn't for me?
The setup call is free, and you pay only when your event goes on sale. If that first event doesn't go well, email Paul within 30 days of paying and he'll refund the {{ price }}.
