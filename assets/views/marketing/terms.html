Price and payment

CrabGate costs {{ price }} a year, taxes included. You pay when you put your first event on sale, by card through Stripe. The subscription renews each year until you cancel.

If CrabGate isn't for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment and we'll refund it in full.

Nothing breaks. Your shop keeps working for 14 days while you sort it out. After that the subscription lapses: you can't publish or sell until you pay, but you can still check people in and Admins can export your data until deletion starts.
