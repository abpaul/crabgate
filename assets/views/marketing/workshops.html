Questions from people who run workshops

Can buyers choose tickets on my website?
Yes. Embed ticket selection for an Event or a Series on your website. Buyers choose a published future date for a Series, then ticket types and quantities. Buyer details, questions, booking terms, and Stripe payment continue through hosted checkout outside the iframe. Each purchase covers one date.
How do repeat dates work?
Set a weekly, fortnightly, or monthly Series and preview its dates. CrabGate creates dates ahead on a rolling eight-date horizon, subject to the Series end date. Each date has separate capacity and orders. Blocked or invalid dates stay visible for review; after fixing them, you must explicitly publish them.
Who receives workshop messages?
Each unique buyer email with a valid ticket receives the event message once, including free and complimentary orders. Fully refunded and void orders are excluded. Buyers forward instructions to their guests; messages do not go to each attendee separately.
How do monthly dates work?
Monthly dates follow the weekday position, such as the second Thursday. If the first date is the fifth Thursday, later dates use the last Thursday of each month.
What happens if I change or stop a Series?
Using an event’s setup for the Series affects only dates not yet created. Stopping the Series stops new dates; it leaves existing dates and orders intact.
What does it cost besides {{ price }}?
Only your card processor's fee. Today that's Stripe, and we add nothing to it.
Can one person pay for several places?
Yes. A buyer can put several places in one order and change the attendee names on the order page until the workshop starts.
What if someone can't come?
You can refund their order from the Back Office in a couple of clicks.
What if it isn't for me?
The setup call is free, and you pay only when your event goes on sale. If that first event doesn't go well, email Paul within 30 days of paying and he'll refund the {{ price }}.
What if my payment to CrabGate fails?
Your shop keeps working for a 14-day grace period, so you have time to sort it out. If payment is not restored, your subscription lapses and ticket sales stop. Export your data before we delete your live hosted data, 90 days after your subscription lapses. While we retain your data, you can check in eligible tickets before the event ends.
