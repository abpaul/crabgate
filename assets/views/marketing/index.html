From your first event to the final export

  1. 1

    Create an event

    Add a date, venue, ticket types, prices, capacity, and sales windows.

  2. 2

    Sell tickets

    Share your hosted shop link. Buyers choose tickets and pay through Stripe, with no account to create.

  3. 3

    Manage attendees

    See who's coming, review their details, and issue complimentary tickets from the Back Office.

  4. 4

    Communicate with buyers

    Preview an event message and its recipient count. Email each eligible buyer once, including free and complimentary orders. Buyers forward it to their guests.

  5. 5

    Check in

    Scan tickets with a phone camera or find attendees by name. Give helpers their own Door Link.

  6. 6

    Export your records

    Download orders, tickets, and check-ins as CSV for your own records.

Why I built CrabGate

Hi, I'm Paul.

Selling a ticket isn't hard. Someone pays, they get a code, and you check the code at the door. Somewhere along the way that turned into a fee on every seat, paid by you or by the people who came to see you.

That fee makes sense when a platform finds your audience for you. It makes a lot less sense when you found them yourself: your list, your regulars, your community.

So I built CrabGate. One flat price a year. The money goes straight to your account. A shop that sells the tickets and a door that checks people in, and nothing you don't need.
