Questions about the price

Is there a free trial?
Better: Paul sets up your next event with you on a free 45-minute call. You pay {{ price }} for the year when you put that event on sale.
Can I get my money back?
Yes. If your first event on CrabGate doesn't go well, email Paul within 30 days of paying and he'll refund the {{ price }}.
Is CrabGate free for community organisations?
Yes. Charities, nonprofits, and community organisations get free access. Email hello@crabgate.com and we'll arrange access with you.
Why US dollars?
We have one price everywhere. Your card provider converts {{ price }} into your currency.
Can I use my own domain, logo, and colours?
Not yet. They're on our list. If they matter to you, tell us.
What if a subscription payment fails?
Nothing breaks. Your shop keeps working for a 14-day grace period, so you have time to sort it out. After grace ends, you can still check people in, and Admins can export data until deletion starts 90 days after the subscription lapses.
Can I start without the call?
Yes. Sign up and set things up yourself whenever you like. You start your subscription when you publish your first event.
