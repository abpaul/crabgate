# No white-label: every Shop has CrabGate's look

Every Shop uses CrabGate's own design and shows only its Organizer's name. Organizers cannot set a logo, colors, fonts, or a custom domain; Hosted Shops live on CrabGate subdomains. This reverses the original goal of white-label branding. We chose it because one strong, Basecamp-inspired design is simpler to build, test, and keep consistent than a neutral frame that must look good in any Organizer's colors.

## Consequences

- There is no Branding settings screen, no per-Organizer theme, and no contrast checking of Organizer colors.
- The Hosted Edition needs no on-demand TLS for custom domains. A single wildcard certificate covers every Shop.

## Direction

On 2026-10-02 we decided that this is the state for now, not for good. An Organizer's own domain, logo, and colours on a Hosted Shop are planned for later and have no date. Until they ship, every page that describes the Shop says what it has today and says that these are on our list. No page gives a date.
